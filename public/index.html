How it works

🌐

Capture

A real browser visits every page of your site, downloading all HTML, CSS, JS, images, and fonts into a self-contained copy.

⛓

Pin

The copy is uploaded to Filecoin Onchain Cloud -- content-addressed, redundant, and stored on the Filecoin network.

🔗

Serve

Your site gets an IPFS CID and is instantly accessible through gateways worldwide. Optionally point an ENS domain at it.
