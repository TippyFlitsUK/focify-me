Paste a list of CIDs into the checker and it tells you how many are ready to move and builds a prompt with your list in it. Give that prompt to a coding agent and it reads the runbook, measures each item, and migrates it.

Checking and estimating are free and run in your browser. The migration itself spends from a wallet you fund for the purpose.
