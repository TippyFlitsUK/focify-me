The audit record of a public model-training run, with some of its large checkpoint files copied to a storage provider that has to prove, on chain and on a schedule, that it still holds them.

The page shows which parts of the record have a copy on Filecoin Onchain Cloud, the data set that holds them and how the files were split into pieces. It is a snapshot on the calibration testnet.
